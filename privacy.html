Privacy policy

SkyScout is operated by Russell Hanson. This policy covers the current iPhone and Apple Watch ground-research and replay implementation, including internal TestFlight build 0.1.0 (1).

Records stay on your device

SkyScout stores session records in the app’s local storage. A ground session you start can contain precise location, GPS elevation and accuracy, pressure, relative height, timestamps, and interruptions. Imported GPX files can contain precise tracks and timestamps; the original import is retained alongside the local session. Synthetic examples are labeled separately.

The current app has no SkyScout account, advertising, tracking SDK, analytics service, cloud-upload endpoint, or Watch–iPhone transfer. Russell Hanson does not receive your recordings through the app. The app does not request HealthKit data or collect heart rate.

Your permissions and choices

Browsing examples and importing GPX files do not require sensor permissions. Starting ground research requests access to available location and motion data. You can change these permissions in your device’s Settings. Denying access does not prevent browsing stored sessions.

You choose whether to export a file using the system share interface. An export may contain precise tracks and times. Any person or service you select handles the copy under its own practices. SkyScout does not automatically send a copy to its operator.

Retention and deletion

Local sessions remain until you delete them or remove the app’s data. On iPhone, deleting a session through its confirmation flow removes the app’s local session, retained import, and app-owned prepared export copies for that session. Copies previously shared elsewhere remain outside this action. Watch records have separate local storage; removing the Watch app removes its local app data, subject to Apple’s backup and restore behavior.

Device backups, app removal, and restoration are controlled by Apple and your device/account settings. Deleting one device copy does not erase a backup or a copy someone else retained.

Support messages

If you email support, Russell Hanson receives your email address and the information you choose to include and uses it to investigate and answer your request. Avoid sending precise coordinates or other unnecessary personal information. Support correspondence may be retained while resolving a request and maintaining a record of that assistance. Contact us to request deletion; legal obligations or ongoing issues may require retention.

Website and platform services

This support website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process ordinary web-request information, such as an IP address, under GitHub’s privacy statement. These pages include no advertising, analytics scripts, or third-party embeds.

App Store, TestFlight, system sharing, and device backups are Apple or user-selected services. Apple handles its own platform diagnostics and TestFlight feedback under its privacy practices. Information you deliberately submit through TestFlight may be provided to the developer by Apple.

Updates and contact

This policy will be updated when SkyScout’s data handling changes. Features that add transfers, online services, or health integration require a revised policy and appropriate App Store disclosures before release.

For privacy questions or requests, email russellhanson@gmail.com.
