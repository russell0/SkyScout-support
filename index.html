Help with airplane GPS, GPX imports, session replay, and the Apple Watch companion.

Current availability

SkyScout is in development. Build 0.1.0 (1) is an internal TestFlight preview for historical track review and foreground ground research. It is not available on the public App Store.

Version 1.0 (2) adds a supplemental airplane GPS display and recording, a background location session and paired-device recording copies. Physical aircraft accuracy, wrist-down continuity and battery endurance remain unverified. Neither build provides live skydiving altitude, deployment alarms, offline terrain or airspace, or an emergency service. Keep your aircraft and sport instruments.

Get in touch

Email russellhanson@gmail.com. Include your app version, device model, operating-system version, and the steps that caused the issue. Say whether the session was imported, synthetic, ground research, or airplane GPS.

Please keep precise tracks and private coordinates out of your initial support report. Share a recording only if you deliberately choose to do so.

Import and replay

On iPhone, choose Import GPX from Files, then open the imported session in your library. SkyScout retains the original imported GPX file. Missing timestamps, locations, and reference information remain unknown; replay does not reconstruct measurements that were never recorded.

The synthetic paraglide, jump and airplane examples let you explore without location or motion permissions. Their observations are made up and are labeled as demos.

Airplane GPS

In version 1.0 (2), choose Start airplane GPS on Watch or Open airplane instrument on iPhone. Read the GPS explanation and start recording. The display shows knots, GPS altitude in feet, true track, a slow GPS climb trend and recording time. Swipe to the Watch control page to end and save.

Ground speed is not airspeed, and track is not aircraft heading. Cabin pressure is kept separate from GPS altitude. Stale or unavailable GPS readings become dashes; recording gaps remain visible on replay. The climb trend summarizes 10–20 seconds and is not instantaneous VSI. On a dimmed Watch, raise your wrist to see current readings.

Ground recording

Recording requires a separate start and acknowledgement. Allow location and motion access when prompted if you want to collect available sensor observations. Sensor collection pauses when the app is inactive; interruptions are marked in the record. Keep normal instruments for any flight activity.

In build 0.1.0 (1), phone and Watch records stay on the device where they were created. Version 1.0 (2) queues finished Watch recordings to the paired iPhone and adds Send and Retry controls for saved sessions. Open both apps once to establish their connection. A confirmation refers to a verified past save, not proof that the other device still retains the copy.

Manage your records

On iPhone, open a session to share an export or delete its local record. Confirm deletion only after retaining any copy you need. Deleting a local record does not delete files you already exported or copies in device backups.
