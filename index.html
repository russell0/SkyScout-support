Help with airplane GPS, GPX imports, session replay, and the Apple Watch companion.

Current availability

SkyScout is available for owner testing through TestFlight. Its first public release is being prepared for aviation GPS recording and replay; it is not yet available on the public App Store.

The aviation release keeps six Crown-controlled Watch displays and personal flight recordings. It does not provide operational charts, airspace guidance, terrain clearance, traffic or flight alerts. Use your aircraft instruments for flying decisions. Aircraft accuracy and battery endurance are not claimed.

Get in touch

Email russellhanson@gmail.com. Include your app version, device model, operating-system version, and the steps that caused the issue. Say whether the session was imported, synthetic or recorded with GPS. Older research records remain supported for review and export.

Please keep precise tracks and private coordinates out of your initial support report. Share a recording only if you deliberately choose to do so.

Import and replay

On iPhone, choose Import GPX from Files, then open the imported session in your library. SkyScout retains the original imported GPX file. Missing timestamps, locations, and reference information remain unknown; replay does not reconstruct measurements that were never recorded.

Choose Demo airplane to explore without location or motion permissions. Demo observations are made up and clearly labeled. Existing records keep their original mode and provenance.

Airplane GPS

Choose Start airplane GPS on Watch or Open airplane instrument on iPhone. Read the GPS explanation and start recording. The displays show GPS ground speed in knots, GPS altitude in feet, true ground track, a slow GPS height trend and recording time. On Watch, turn the Crown or swipe vertically between Focus, Vertical, Altitude, Track, Pressure and Session. End & save is on Session.

Ground speed is not airspeed, and true track is not aircraft heading. GPS altitude is not barometric altitude or height above terrain. Cabin pressure stays separate. Source age, available accuracy and recording gaps matter when reviewing a measurement. The GPS height trend summarizes 10–20 seconds and is not instantaneous VSI. Raise your wrist to see current readings.

Recording and paired copies

Recording starts only when you choose to start. Allow location and motion access when prompted to collect available GPS and pressure observations. Airplane recording requests background location until you finish; actual delivery depends on the device and system. Keep interruptions and gaps in mind when reviewing the saved record.

Finished Watch recordings queue a copy to the paired iPhone. Open SkyScout on both devices, and use Retry paired transfers if a copy is pending. Saved sessions also have Send controls. The sender retains its original, and confirmation follows a validated save on the receiver. A past confirmation does not prove the other device still retains that copy.

Manage your records

On iPhone, open a session to share an export or delete its local record. Confirm deletion only after retaining any copy you need. Deleting a local record does not delete copies on the other device, files you already exported or copies in device backups.
